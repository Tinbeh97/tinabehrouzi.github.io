Tina Behrouzi

Welcome to my personal profile! I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Toronto and the Vector Institute, working under the supervision of Anna Goldenberg. My research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence and healthcare, with a focus on time-series analysis, self-supervised learning, and graph machine learning.
